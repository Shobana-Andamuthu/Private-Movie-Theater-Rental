The Manifesto

Why Public Theaters Lost Their Magic

Public multiplexes suffer from ringing phones, noisy crowds, and commercialized popcorn. Living rooms lack the physical chest-thumping impact of reference Dolby Atmos.

VELLORA was engineered as the solution: an intimate architectural haven where film lovers, couples, families, and friends can immerse themselves in uncompressed 4K video, studio acoustic isolation, and silent in-seat dining.

Acoustical Purity

The Four Pillars of Vellora Engineering

Every room is certified to DCI-P3 color gamuts and STC-65 sound transmission standards.

4K RGB Laser Engines

Zero bulb degradation. Crisp 50,000:1 contrast ratios that make deep blacks disappear into darkness.

Atmos 3D Soundstage

Dedicated ceiling height drivers and sub-bass seat exciters tuned for lossless object-based audio.

Floating Room Isolation

Decoupled wall studs and dual acoustic glass doors guarantee 0 dB crosstalk between suites.

Smart Starlight Ambiance

Fiber-optic constellation ceilings and hidden floor runner LEDs dim automatically at showtime.

Health & Sanctuary

Hospital-Grade Clean Air & Sanitization

Because privacy should mean pure peace of mind. Every suite features dedicated ventilation and medical-grade sanitization between bookings.

Air is never recirculated between suites. Each room receives a full 100% fresh atmospheric air flush every 15 minutes.

Velvet recliners and upholstery are sanitized with medical steam and UV-C cycles during the 45-minute turnover window.
